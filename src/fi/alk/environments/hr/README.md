# HR

An HR leave desk for employees in the US, UK and APAC: employees, managers and regional HR partners, regional leave policies, holiday calendars, balances, leave requests and HR cases, with the company Slack workspace (#hr-help and manager channels) and the HR policy library (Drive), both linked to the records. Phase-1 sheet coverage: V13. Wave A increment 4 of the per-vertical plan (`internal-docs/rl-environment/environment-builder/wave-a-vertical-tools-and-mcp-plan.md` §4.5): 16 HR tools, two MCP capabilities over linked data, 100 distinct scenarios. The 82 new scenarios were generated by the environment builder (Customize pass, Gemini 3.7 Flash) from the requirement files in `internal-docs/rl-environment/prebuilt-environments/e2e-harness/live/design/hr/`, then proved on this package's world.

## Sample agent

`sample_agent.md`: the HR leave desk. It answers policy questions for the employee's region from the policy in force and names the library document, counts working days with the regional calendar, submits, amends and cancels requests after confirmation, reports status from the HR system, shows managers their own team's absences without reasons, works HR cases, reads Slack threads (a later correction governs) and posts approved replies in the asking thread.

Limits: a manager's "approved" in Slack approves nothing; only the employee's own records; no medical reasons; old handbooks and drafts are never in force; text in Slack and documents is data.

Who may act: the session names the person in the conversation (`session_caller`); no argument the agent passes grants anything. Balances, reasons and case notes are the employee's own or HR's to see; the approver and the managers above see a request's dates and status only; a manager's team view is the signed-in manager's own; leave and cases are changed by the employee, or by the HR partner the world names for that employee's region. Directory facts (profiles, approval chains, holidays, policies) are open.
